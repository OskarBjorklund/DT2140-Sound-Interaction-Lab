    *Interaction 1: Shake → Bells* Shaking the device triggers a bell sound. This mapping mimics the natural action of shaking a handheld bell.

    *Interaction 2: Tilt → Brass* Tilting the device controls the air pressure of a brass instrument, continuously changing its sound. This allows intuitive control through physical movement.

    *Interaction 3: Acceleration → Thunder* A thunder sound is triggered when the device's filtered acceleration exceeds 5 m/s². Small movements are ignored, while strong movements trigger thunder, with a cooldown to prevent repeated activation.
